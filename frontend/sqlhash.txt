Step 1: What does VARCHAR do?
password_hash VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL


This only creates a column that can store text.
For example, it can store:
- Rahul@123
- Hello123
- $2b$10$abc... (a password hash)
It doesn't automatically change one into another.
